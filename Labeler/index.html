Keys Every tile needs a box and nothing else does. Click empty space to add a box the average size, or drag to draw one; click one and ⌫ to delete it; drag a box to move it, its corner to resize. Enter marks the capture done and opens the next. Values are optional: click a box, type its tile (r12, j) and Enter. ←→ turn which way is up. +− zoom. [] previous and next capture.

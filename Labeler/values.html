Keys
Each tile is drawn upright by its orientation, so one upside down or sideways has the wrong orientation. Yellow is unconfirmed, least sure first, and green confirmed. Click a tile to select it, shift-click to select a run, cmd-click to add or remove one, or a to select every unconfirmed one. Type a tile (r6, j) and Enter to set and confirm the selected ones, ? for ones you can't read, or just Enter to confirm what they show. ←→ turn their orientation, x marks them background (dimmed: the table behind a rack), Esc selects none. Double-click opens the capture. Changes save as you make them.

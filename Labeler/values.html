Keys
Each tile is drawn turned upright by its orientation, so one upside down or sideways has the wrong orientation. Yellow is unconfirmed, least sure first, green confirmed. Click a tile to pick it, shift-click to pick a run, a to pick every unconfirmed one. ←→ turn the picked ones. Type a tile (r6, j) and Enter to relabel and confirm them, or just Enter to confirm them as they are. Esc picks none. Double-click opens the capture in the labeler. Changes save as you make them.
